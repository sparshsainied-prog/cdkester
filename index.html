The writer

Spooky stories since sixth grade

C. D. Kester lives in Kingwood, Texas. His first spooky story was a retelling of his mother's real encounter with a UFO. By thirteen he was working on his first novel, and it had a scarecrow in it.

By day he is a metal recycling professional, and he is a husband and a father of two.

"I began writing spooky stories when I was in sixth grade."From his blog, A Writer's Journey, April 2024
